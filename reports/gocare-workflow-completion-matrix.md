- **Application User.** The saved web session establishes one visible menu set. The seven role scenarios still need securely authorized accounts and expected entitlements.

- **Password length.** The Change Password form is accessible. Three password inputs have an HTML maximum of 15 and client validator hooks. Server policy, edit cancellation and changed-account behavior remain open.
- **ERAMET MET configuration.** The shared Company MET and Global MET screens are accessible. No ERAMET-specific mapping, employee assignment or expiry outcome was exercised.
- **MD-804.** Existing expectations specify mail content and absence of delivery. The events and exact templates require a synthetic recipient and isolated sink.
